## What maintainers do

- Review and merge pull requests (one maintainer approval; a maintainer's own PR needs another maintainer when there is one).
- Triage issues, cut releases (`git tag vX.Y.Z`) and handle security reports (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).
- Keep the scope small: Orbitgate filters namespace lists and nothing else. Features that belong to the apiserver (auth, RBAC, admission) are out of scope.

## Becoming a maintainer

Regular, quality contributions and reviews over a few months. An existing maintainer proposes you in a PR to this file, and the other maintainers agree. Maintainers who are inactive for a year move to Emeritus.
